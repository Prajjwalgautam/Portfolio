What does an AI engineer do?

An AI engineer turns AI models into working software. That means choosing the right model, writing and testing prompts, connecting the model to your documents, products or customer data, building the interface around it, and keeping costs and errors under control.

For a small or medium business this usually looks like a support assistant that answers from your own content, an internal tool that drafts documents or reports, an automation that moves data between apps, or AI-assisted content production for marketing.
