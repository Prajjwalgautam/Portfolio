(Overview)

Lead generation means bringing in people who have raised their hand: they filled a form, sent a message or called. For real estate, education, clinics, travel and services in Nepal, that is usually what drives sales.

I build the whole path: the ad, the landing page or form, the instant WhatsApp or call follow-up, and the tracking that shows which ad brought which lead. Because I am also a developer, I can build the landing page and connect the form to your sheet or CRM myself.

How does lead generation work?

A lead generation system has four parts. Traffic: ads on Facebook, Instagram or Google, plus SEO and social posts. Capture: a fast landing page, an instant form or a WhatsApp click. Follow-up: a reply within minutes, by message or call. Measurement: cost per lead, lead quality and how many leads turn into sales.

Most campaigns fail at follow-up or measurement, not at the ad. Fixing those two often matters more than raising the budget.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good cost per lead in Nepal?

+

It depends on the industry, the offer and the area, so be wary of anyone quoting a number before seeing your business. The useful measure is cost per sale: a cheap lead that never buys is expensive.

Facebook lead ads or Google Ads?

+

Facebook and Instagram are better for creating demand and reach at a low cost. Google Ads catch people who are already searching. Many businesses use both.

Why do I get leads but no sales?

+

Usually slow follow-up, a weak offer or the wrong audience. I check all three before changing the budget.

Can leads come straight to my WhatsApp?

+

Yes. Click-to-WhatsApp ads and form alerts can send leads to WhatsApp, email or a shared sheet within seconds.

How many leads can I expect?

+

It depends on your budget, offer and market, so I do not promise a number. After the first two to four weeks we have real figures and a clear plan to improve them.

Do you call or qualify the leads?

+

I set up the follow-up system, templates and alerts; your team makes the calls, because they know the product best.

Is lead generation good for real estate in Nepal?

+

Yes. Property is a high-value, considered purchase, so a steady flow of serious enquiries matters more than reach. It is the core of the marketing I do for Nepal Bhoomi.
