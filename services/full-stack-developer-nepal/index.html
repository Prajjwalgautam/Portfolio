Full-stack means you deal with one person for the design, the frontend, the backend, the database and the launch. Fewer hand-offs, faster fixes, and one person who understands the whole system.

What does a full-stack developer do?

A full-stack developer builds both sides of a web product: the part people see in the browser (frontend) and the server, database and logic behind it (backend).

For a business that means one developer can take an idea from a sketch to a live website or web app with logins, dashboards, payments and reports.
