(Overview)

Jewellers compete on how a piece looks in a three-second scroll. Light, colour, motion and sound decide whether a necklace feels precious or ordinary.

I bring the same tools I use daily for video and 3D work: colour grading, motion graphics, Blender 3D and paid ads, focused on gold, diamond and silver jewellery.

What does jewellery marketing include?

It combines product content (macro video, photography edits, 3D renders), a catalogue customers can browse, and campaigns timed to wedding season, Teej, Tihar and Dashain.

Online, it also means trust signals: certification details, clear pricing logic, reviews and fast replies on WhatsApp and Messenger.
