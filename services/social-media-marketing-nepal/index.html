(Overview)

In Nepal, most customers discover a business on Facebook, Instagram or TikTok before they visit a website. A page that posts rarely, replies slowly or looks different every week loses them to a competitor that shows up every day.

I manage social media the way I do for Nepal Bhoomi: a monthly content plan, short videos and graphics made in-house, quick replies, ads for reach and leads, and a report at the end of each month.

What does social media marketing include?

Social media marketing covers strategy (who you are talking to and why), content (videos, carousels, stories and graphics), posting, community management (comments, messages and reviews), paid ads and reporting.

Boosting random posts is not a strategy. Good social media ties each post to a goal: awareness, engagement, enquiries or sales, and measures it.

Frequently asked questions

Which social media platform is best for business in Nepal?

+

Facebook still reaches the widest audience in Nepal, Instagram suits visual brands and younger buyers, and TikTok works well for short, entertaining video. Most businesses do best with two platforms done well rather than four done badly.

How often should a business post on social media?

+

Consistency matters more than volume. For most businesses, three to five good posts a week plus stories is a strong start.
