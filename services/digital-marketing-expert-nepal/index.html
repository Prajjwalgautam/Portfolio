Digital marketing is how a business gets found, trusted and chosen online. For most businesses in Nepal that means social media, ads on Facebook, Instagram and Google, a website that turns visitors into enquiries, and quick replies on WhatsApp.

I run this for real brands. At Nepal Bhoomi I plan and make the content, run social media, track ads and enquiries, handle SEO and audience research, and report on results every month. I offer the same full service to other businesses, in Nepal and abroad.

What does a digital marketing expert do?

A digital marketing expert plans where your customers are, what to say to them and how to measure it, then runs the work: content, social media, ads, search and email. The goal is not likes. It is enquiries, bookings and sales.

Most businesses hire an agency, a designer, an ads person and a developer separately, and then the pieces do not connect. I cover all of it, so the ad, the page it lands on and the follow-up message are planned together.
