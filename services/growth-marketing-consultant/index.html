I do all four. I build websites and apps, produce video daily, run SEO and ads, and present monthly reports at Nepal Bhoomi.

What does a growth marketing consultant do?

A growth marketer finds the fastest path from attention to revenue. That means researching the audience, choosing channels, running experiments and fixing the weak step in the funnel, whether it is the ad, the page or the offer.

For e-commerce and service businesses this usually covers SEO, paid ads, social video, email and conversion improvements on the website.
