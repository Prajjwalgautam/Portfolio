(Overview)

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) are the next layer of SEO. Instead of ten blue links, AI assistants read the web and write one answer. If your business is not clear, consistent and quotable, it is left out.

I am a developer and SEO specialist, so I work on both sides: the technical signals AI crawlers read, and the content they quote. This portfolio itself is built that way, with structured data on every page, an llms.txt file and answer-first FAQs.

What is AEO, and how is it different from SEO?

SEO helps a page rank in search results. AEO helps your business get named inside the answer itself: in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and voice assistants.

AI engines favour sources that state facts clearly, use structured data, are mentioned consistently across trusted sites and answer real questions directly. AEO work builds exactly those signals.

Frequently asked questions

What does AEO stand for?

+

Answer Engine Optimization: making your business the answer AI assistants and search engines give, not just a link they list.

Is AEO different from GEO?

+

They overlap. GEO, Generative Engine Optimization, focuses on generative AI tools like ChatGPT; AEO also covers featured snippets and voice answers. I do both together.

Does AEO replace SEO?

+

No. AI engines still rely on well-ranked, technically sound pages. AEO is built on top of good SEO.

How do you measure AEO?

+

I track a fixed set of prompts across major AI assistants each month and report where your business is mentioned or cited.

What is GEO?

+

GEO, Generative Engine Optimization, makes AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity recognise and recommend your brand. It works by making the facts about you clear, consistent and repeated across trusted sites.

Is AEO replacing SEO?

+

No. AI answers often draw on pages that already rank well, so SEO and AEO work together.

How do you measure AEO results?

+

Each month I ask the main AI tools a fixed set of buyer questions and record whether you are named and which page is cited.
