What does real estate marketing include?

Good property marketing does three jobs: show the property beautifully, build trust in the agent or developer, and turn interest into enquiries.

In Nepal that means short vertical videos for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, clear listings with location, area in aana and ropani and price, and fast follow-up on every lead.
