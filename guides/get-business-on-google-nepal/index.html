How to get your business on Google in Nepal

Short answerVerify your website in Google Search Console, submit your sitemap, request indexing for your main pages, and create a Google Business Profile. Then import the site into Bing Webmaster Tools. All of this is free and takes about an hour; your pages usually start appearing within days.

By Prajwal Gautam · AI engineer and full-stack developer, Kathmandu · Updated November 2026

Step 1: Check whether Google already knows your site

Search Google for site:yourdomain.com. If nothing appears, Google has not indexed your site yet, and no amount of keyword work will help until it does.

Step 2: Verify your site in Google Search Console

Go to search.google.com/search-console, add your site as a URL-prefix property, and verify it. The simplest method is uploading the small HTML file Google gives you to the root of your site. Keep that file there permanently, because Google rechecks it.

Step 3: Submit your sitemap and request indexing

Your sitemap.xml lists every page you want found. Submit it under Sitemaps. Then use URL inspection on your most important pages, such as your home page and main service pages, and click Request indexing. Requesting the same page again does not speed it up.

Step 4: Add Bing, which also feeds AI search

In Bing Webmaster Tools, choose Import from Google Search Console. It copies your site and sitemap in one click. Bing results power Microsoft Copilot and DuckDuckGo and feed into ChatGPT search, so this step matters for AI visibility too.

If your site supports IndexNow, it can notify Bing and other engines the moment a page changes.

Step 5: Create a Google Business Profile

A Google Business Profile puts you on Google Maps and in "near me" searches. Service businesses without a shop can choose a service area such as the Kathmandu Valley instead of showing a street address. Use the same name, phone number and website as on your site.

Step 6: Fix what stops pages from ranking

  1. One clear H1 per page that uses the words people search for.
  2. A page title under about 60 characters, starting with the main keyword.
  3. A meta description that answers the search in one or two sentences.
  4. Fast loading on mobile data, with compressed images.
  5. Real text that Google can read, not only images or animations.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to appear on Google in Nepal?

+

After you submit a sitemap and request indexing, pages usually appear within a few days to two weeks. Ranking well for competitive searches takes longer.

Do I have to pay Google to appear in search?

+

No. Organic search listings are free. Google Ads are a separate, paid option.

Does a .com.np domain rank in Nepal?

+

Yes. A .np domain is a clear local signal, and it ranks for searches worldwide too.
