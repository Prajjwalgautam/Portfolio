How to get your business on Google in Nepal

Short answerCreate and verify a free Google Business Profile so you appear on Google Maps, then verify your website in Google Search Console and submit your sitemap. Keep your name, address and phone number the same everywhere, ask customers for reviews, and import your site into Bing Webmaster Tools. All of this is free.

By Prajwal Gautam · AI engineer and digital marketing expert, Kathmandu · Updated November 2026

Step 1: Check whether Google already knows you

Search Google for your business name, and for site:yourdomain.com if you have a website. Also search Google Maps. If nothing appears, Google has not found you yet, and keyword work will not help until it does.

Step 2: Create your Google Business Profile (Google My Business)

Google Business Profile, still often called Google My Business or GMB, is what puts you on Google Maps and in the local "3-pack" for searches like "property agent near me" or "web developer Kathmandu". Go to google.com/business, search for your business name, and claim it if it already exists or create it if not.

Choose the most accurate primary category, then add secondary categories. Service businesses without a shop can set a service area such as the Kathmandu Valley instead of showing a street address.

Step 3: Verify your profile

Google asks you to verify before the profile goes fully live. Depending on your business, it may offer a phone or text code, email, a short video of your premises, or a postcard. Use the options Google shows you; a video usually needs to show your signboard, your workspace and proof you manage the business.

Step 4: Fill in everything and add photos

Add your hours, phone number (a mobile number you also use on WhatsApp works), website, services or products, and a description that says what you do and where. Upload real photos of your office, team and work. Complete profiles get more clicks and calls.

Step 5: Get reviews and reply to them

Reviews are one of the strongest local ranking signals. Ask happy customers for a review with your short review link, and reply to every review, good or bad, politely. Recent reviews count more than old ones.

Step 6: Keep your NAP consistent

NAP means name, address and phone number. Use exactly the same details on your website, Google Business Profile, Facebook page and Nepali directories. Mismatched details confuse Google and AI tools about who you are.

Step 7: Verify your website in Search Console and Bing

In Google Search Console, add your site and verify it, for example by uploading the small HTML file Google gives you to your site’s root. Keep that file there permanently. Submit your sitemap.xml and request indexing for your most important pages.

Then open Bing Webmaster Tools and choose Import from Google Search Console. Bing powers Microsoft Copilot and DuckDuckGo results and feeds ChatGPT search, so this matters for AI visibility too.

Step 8: Fix what stops pages from ranking

  1. One clear H1 per page that uses the words people search for, including your city.
  2. A page title under about 60 characters, starting with the main keyword.
  3. A meta description that answers the search in one or two sentences.
  4. Fast loading on mobile data, with compressed images.
  5. Real text Google can read, not only images, and a page for each main service or area.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to appear on Google in Nepal?

+

A verified Google Business Profile can appear on Maps within days. Website pages usually appear within a few days to two weeks after you submit a sitemap. Ranking well for competitive searches takes longer.

Is Google My Business free in Nepal?

+

Yes. Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is free. Google Ads are a separate, paid option.

Can I use a WhatsApp number on Google Business Profile?

+

Yes. Use a phone number customers can call; a mobile number you also use for WhatsApp works, and you can link WhatsApp in your website and posts.

Should I put keywords in my business name?

+

No. Use your real business name. Adding keywords that are not part of your real name breaks Google’s guidelines and can get the profile suspended.

Does a .com.np domain rank in Nepal?

+

Yes. A .np domain is a clear local signal and still ranks for searches worldwide.
