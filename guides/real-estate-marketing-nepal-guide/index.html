Real estate marketing in Nepal: what works online

Short answerIn Nepal, property sells online through short videos on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, a fast listing website with clear price and location, Facebook and Instagram lead ads, quick replies on WhatsApp Business, and local SEO for areas such as Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur. Track every lead back to the ad that brought it.

By Prajwal Gautam · AI engineer and digital marketing expert, Kathmandu · Updated November 2026

Start with a professional property website

Social media brings attention; a website keeps it. Every listing needs its own fast page with price, area, road access, map location, photos, video and a one-tap WhatsApp button. Slow pages lose buyers on mobile data. A website also ranks on Google and builds trust when social reach drops.

Video comes first on social media

Buyers scroll before they search. A 30 to 60 second property video with the price, location and three best features does more than a gallery of photos. Consistent branding, such as the same intro, captions and a short sound logo, makes a series recognisable.

Building videos partly in code, as with Remotion, lets a small team publish often without starting from a blank timeline each time. That is how Nepal Bhoomi produces its property reels.

Facebook, Instagram and Google ads

Facebook and Instagram lead ads work well for property because the form is pre-filled and quick, and you can target by location and interests. Google Ads catch people actively searching, such as "land for sale in Kathmandu". Start small, then move budget to the ads that bring real site visits, not just clicks.

WhatsApp Business for real estate

Most property conversations in Nepal end up on WhatsApp. Use WhatsApp Business for a business profile, quick replies and labels for each lead. Broadcast lists and Status updates share new listings with interested buyers who have saved your number. Reply within minutes: slow replies lose leads.

Local SEO for Kathmandu Valley areas

People search by place: "land for sale in Budhanilkantha", "house for rent in Lalitpur". Area pages, listing titles and video titles that use these place names, plus a Google Business Profile with reviews, catch these searches.

Measure what brings sales

  1. Leads per week, by channel (ads, website, WhatsApp, walk-ins).
  2. Cost per lead and cost per site visit for each ad.
  3. Reply time on WhatsApp and calls.
  4. Which areas, property types and videos bring the most enquiries.
  5. A one-page monthly report the owner can read in five minutes.

Trust and security

Buyers and sellers share phone numbers and documents. A property platform should protect them: secure sign-in, two-factor authentication for staff and an audit log of changes. The Nepal Bhoomi platform was built this way.

Frequently asked questions

Which digital platform is most effective for real estate in Nepal?

+

Facebook and Instagram reach the most buyers, and WhatsApp is where deals are discussed. A listing website and Google searches bring serious buyers. The best results come from using them together.

How can local SEO help Nepali real estate agents?

+

Local SEO puts your listings and Google Business Profile in front of people searching for property in a specific area, such as Lalitpur or Bhaktapur, at the moment they are looking.

Do I need a website if I sell on Facebook?

+

Yes. A website with listings ranks on Google, builds trust and keeps your enquiries coming when social reach drops.
