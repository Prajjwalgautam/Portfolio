How to hire an AI engineer in Nepal

Short answerHire an AI engineer in Nepal who can show live, working projects, not only demos. Write a short project brief, ask how they will connect AI to your own data, agree a small first project with a fixed quote, and make sure you own the code, prompts and data at the end.

By Prajwal Gautam · AI engineer and digital marketing expert, Kathmandu · Updated November 2026

What does an AI engineer do?

An AI engineer turns AI models into working software: choosing a model, writing and testing prompts, connecting the model to your documents or customer data through an API, building the screen people use, and keeping running costs and errors under control.

Companies usually hire one for four things: building an MVP of a new AI product, adding AI features to an existing website or app, building AI into a mobile app, or automating repetitive work such as reports, emails and data entry.

What to put in your project brief

A one-page brief gets you faster, more accurate quotes. Include the problem you want solved, who will use it, what data the AI should use, where it will live (your website, an app, WhatsApp or internal tools), your deadline, and how you will know it works.

Questions to ask before you hire

  1. Can you show me a live project, not only a demo or a screenshot?
  2. How will you connect the AI to our data, and what data leaves our systems?
  3. What will it cost to run each month, not only to build?
  4. How will we test that the answers are correct and on-brand?
  5. Who owns the code, the prompts and the data when the project ends?
  6. Can you also build the website or app the feature lives in?

What sets a strong AI engineer apart

Look for someone who ships complete products, not only models: a portfolio with live links, solid web or app development skills, care for security and user data, and plain explanations of trade-offs. An engineer who tells you when AI is the wrong tool is worth more than one who says yes to everything.

How much does it cost to hire an AI engineer in Nepal?

It depends on scope, not on a fixed hourly rate. A focused first project, such as a support assistant trained on your own content, costs far less than a full AI product. Ask for a fixed quote per phase. Rates in Nepal are usually lower than in the US or Europe for the same quality of work.

Freelancer, agency or hiring platform?

A freelance AI engineer is usually faster and cheaper for focused projects, and you speak directly to the person writing the code. An agency suits very large projects that need many people. Hiring platforms help you compare profiles but add fees; hiring directly avoids them.

Start with a small first project

A good first AI project takes about two to six weeks and solves one clear problem. It proves the value before a larger budget is spent. Nepal’s time zone (UTC+5:45) overlaps with clients in Asia, the Middle East, Europe and Australia.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a data scientist or an AI engineer?

+

For most business projects, an AI engineer. Data scientists research and train models; AI engineers use existing models to build products, which is what most businesses need.

What is the difference between hiring a freelance AI engineer and an agency in Nepal?

+

A freelancer is usually faster and cheaper, and you work directly with the developer. An agency adds project managers and more people, which suits large projects.

Can an AI engineer work on my existing website or app?

+

Yes, if they are also a web or app developer. Ask which frameworks they have shipped, such as Laravel, Node.js, React or Next.js.
