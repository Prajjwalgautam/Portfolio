What are AEO and GEO?

Short answerAEO (Answer Engine Optimization) makes your content the answer that AI tools and voice assistants quote. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) makes AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity recognise and recommend your business by name. Both build on SEO, which gets your website ranked in Google and Bing.

By Prajwal Gautam · AI engineer and full-stack developer, Kathmandu · Updated November 2026

SEO, AEO and GEO side by side

Think of them as three goals for the same website. SEO gets you ranked: your page appears when someone searches. AEO gets you quoted: an AI tool lifts your answer into its reply. GEO gets you recommended: when someone asks an AI tool who to hire, your name comes up.

They overlap. AI answers often draw on pages that already rank, so a site with weak SEO rarely wins at AEO or GEO.

TermStands forGoal
SEOSearch Engine OptimizationRanked in Google and Bing results
AEOAnswer Engine OptimizationQuoted in AI answers and featured snippets
GEOGenerative Engine OptimizationRecognised and recommended by name in AI tools

Why this matters for businesses in Nepal now

More buyers now start with a question to an AI tool instead of a list of links: "Who builds real-estate websites in Kathmandu?" or "Best SEO expert in Nepal". The tool picks a few names and pages. If your facts are unclear or missing, it picks someone else.

Many Nepali businesses still have thin websites with no structured data and inconsistent details across Facebook, Google Maps and directories. That makes this an early window: clear, consistent businesses stand out quickly.

How AEO works: write the answer first

AI tools quote short, direct answers. Put a 40 to 60 word answer right under each question heading, then explain. Use the exact wording people search for, such as "How much does a website cost in Kathmandu?", as headings.

Mark up your questions and answers with FAQ structured data, keep pages fast, and make sure crawlers like GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot are allowed in robots.txt.

How GEO works: be the same everywhere

AI tools trust facts they can confirm in several places. Your name, what you do, your city, your website and your best work should read the same on your site, LinkedIn, GitHub, Google Business Profile and any directory.

On your site, structured data (Person or Organization, Service, FAQ) tells machines exactly who you are. An llms.txt file gives AI tools a plain-text summary. A short "disambiguating description" helps when other people share your name.

A simple checklist you can start today

  1. Verify your site in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, and submit your sitemap.
  2. Add an answer-first FAQ to every service page, with FAQ structured data.
  3. Allow AI crawlers in robots.txt and publish an llms.txt summary.
  4. Use one name, one description and one website link on every profile.
  5. Each month, ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini the questions your buyers ask, and note who they name.

Frequently asked questions

Is AEO the same as SEO?

+

No. SEO is about ranking a page in search results. AEO is about having your answer quoted by AI tools and featured snippets. They work best together.

How long do AEO and GEO take?

+

On-site changes take days. Being named by AI tools usually takes one to three months, because the tools need to see consistent facts about you in several trusted places.

Can a small business in Nepal do AEO?

+

Yes. The basics, which are clear answers, structured data and consistent profiles, cost little and matter more than budget.
