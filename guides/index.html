What are AEO and GEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) makes your content the answer that AI tools, featured snippets and voice assistants quote. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) makes AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity cite and recommend your brand by name. Both build on SEO, which gets your website ranked in Google and Bing.

How to get your business on Google in Nepal

Create and verify a free Google Business Profile so you appear on Google Maps, then verify your website in Google Search Console and submit your sitemap. Keep your name, address and phone number the same everywhere, ask customers for reviews, and import your site into Bing Webmaster Tools. All of this is free.

How to hire an AI engineer in Nepal

Hire an AI engineer in Nepal who can show live, working projects, not only demos. Write a short project brief, ask how they will connect AI to your own data, agree a small first project with a fixed quote, and make sure you own the code, prompts and data at the end.

Real estate marketing in Nepal: what works online

In Nepal, property sells online through short videos on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, a fast listing website with clear price and location, Facebook and Instagram lead ads, quick replies on WhatsApp Business, and local SEO for areas such as Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur. Track every lead back to the ad that brought it.

Next.js vs Laravel: which should you choose?

Choose Next.js for fast, SEO-friendly websites and interactive web apps, especially if a React Native mobile app may follow. Choose Laravel for back-end-heavy systems such as admin panels, forms, records and APIs, or when you need low-cost PHP hosting. You can also combine them: a Laravel API with a Next.js front end.
