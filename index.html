What is AEO and do you offer it?

+

AEO, Answer Engine Optimization, makes your business the answer that ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews give. Yes: I add structured data, llms.txt, answer-first content and consistent citations, and track AI mentions monthly.

Which industries do you work with?

+

Real estate (I co-built the Nepal Bhoomi platform), education and study-abroad consultancies, e-commerce and retail, restaurants and travel, and I offer dedicated packages for jewellers and SaaS companies.

Do you make motion design and animation for SaaS?

+

Yes. I build product explainers, UI animations and launch videos, often in Remotion so they can be re-rendered when your product changes, plus Blender 3D and WebGL.

Yes. I build with Next.js, React, Node.js, Laravel and PostgreSQL, add AI features such as language-model integrations, assistants and automations, and secure them with proper sign-in, 2FA and audit logs.

TypeScript and JavaScript with Next.js, React and Node.js; PHP with Laravel; Python for AI and automation; SQL with PostgreSQL and MySQL; and GLSL for WebGL. If your project needs a stack I don't use, I'll tell you before we start.
