Questions

message me

Are you an agency or one person?

One person covering what an agency covers: strategy, content, social media, ads, SEO and reporting, plus the website or app. You always talk to the person doing the work.

How much does it cost?

Marketing is a monthly retainer; websites and apps are a fixed price per project. You get a written quote after a free 20-minute call, before anything starts. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google.

How soon will I see results?

Ads can bring enquiries within days. Social media growth and SEO build over two to six months. The first month is mostly setup and testing.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No honest marketer can. I guarantee a clear plan, steady work and an honest monthly report, and I move budget to what is working.

Do you work with businesses outside Nepal?

Yes. I work remotely with clients worldwide, with good time overlap for Asia, the Middle East, Europe and Australia.
